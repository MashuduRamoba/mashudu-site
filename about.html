I'm Mashudu Ramoba, a Software Developer and IT professional from Midrand, Gauteng. I hold a National Diploma in IT: Software Development from Tshwane University of Technology and have hands-on enterprise experience at Landis+Gyr (EMEA) and Huawei.

I deliver production results — migrating live systems, building Power Automate workflows, managing SharePoint infrastructure, and supporting Microsoft Dynamics 365 with rigorous data validation and QA.

Certified in Azure (AZ-900), the complete Google Cybersecurity programme, and recognised internationally by NASA. I bring technical depth, cross-team communication, and a genuine drive to build things that work.

I'm fluent in 6 languages — English, Afrikaans, Tshivenda, isiZulu, Tsonga, and Tswana — which helps me collaborate effectively across diverse EMEA teams and stakeholders.
